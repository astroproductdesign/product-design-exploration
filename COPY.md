• **Game name:** Gathering Readiness
• **Pill** `[tight]`: Pillar B · Defense

**Framing callout**
• **Title:** You start the game at 100% Sugar.
• **Paragraph 1:** This is the whole idea, and it is set before the mechanic is even explained. The player does not begin clean and try to stay that way — they begin having **already enjoyed** the pineapple tarts, the love letters, the third round of the tray of togetherness. That is the realistic starting point of Chinese New Year, and pretending otherwise would make the game feel like a lecture.
• **Paragraph 2:** So the goal is never abstaining. It is *active defense and recovery* from a state the player is already in — exactly the role Systema plays in a real festive week.

## 06 · SHARED ENGAGEMENT SHELL

• **Chapter number:** 06
• **Chapter label** `[tight]`: Shared engagement shell

• **Headline:** Two games. One shell around them.
• **Lead paragraph:** Smile Swipe and Gathering Readiness are different mechanics answering different pillars — but they run on the same entry point, the same scoring furniture and the same reward loop. One build, one login, one leaderboard, one redemption flow.
• **Body paragraph:** That keeps production cost down, keeps the brand experience consistent across both halves of the message, and means the shell can be reused for the next festival without rebuilding it.

**Flow steps**
• **Step 1 title / body:** Entry / QR on pack, in-store, or social link. One tap in, no app download.
• **Step 2 title / body:** Game / Player picks a mode — Smile Swipe or Gathering Readiness — or plays both.
• **Step 3 title / body:** Result / Score, meters, and the badge earned this session.
• **Step 4 title / body:** Leaderboard & badges / Daily reset plus a running festival-period board. Badge shelf shows the 春联 set.
• **Step 5 title / body:** Share / Result card built for group chats — the couplet blessing doubles as a greeting.
• **Step 6 title / body:** Redeem / Score tiers unlock vouchers, redeemable in-store or at retail partners.

• **Closing note:** Daily leaderboard reset gives a reason to return through all fifteen days; the festival-period board gives the campaign a finish line. Shareable results turn the reward into a CNY greeting people actually want to send — distribution that comes from the culture, not from paid media.
