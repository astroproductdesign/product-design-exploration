Meowpanion My pick

"Meow" + "companion" in one word. It keeps the spirit of Cat Companion, is easy to say, and is unique enough to find in an app store.

Cat Compawnion

The current name with a paw pun in the middle. It's the smallest change from today's title, but players may misspell it when searching.

Neko Companion

"Neko" is Japanese for cat, which suits the art style. One caution: it sits close to Neko Atsume in app stores and searches, so players may confuse the two.

More word-play to consider: Purrpanion, Meow Companion, Nekompanion, My Meowpanion.
