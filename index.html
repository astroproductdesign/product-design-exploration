Systema Oral Care · Lion Mini-game campaign pitch Chinese New Year

One routine. Every season of life.

Oral care isn't a one-time habit — it's something that travels with you through every stage, every celebration, every year. This campaign zooms in on one season in particular: Chinese New Year, when indulgence and connection both peak at once, and your smile is doing more work than usual. The season changes. The routine doesn't have to.

01Brand foundation

Oral care is lifelong. Festive season just raises the stakes.

Most people treat oral care as background maintenance — brush, rinse, forget about it until something hurts. Systema's position is that it's closer to a daily companion: it should adapt with you, not sit in the same routine your whole life.

Festive season is where that idea gets tested hardest, in two directions at once. It's a season of indulgence — more sweet treats than any ordinary week. And it's a season of closeness — more conversations, more greetings, more photos, all at close range. Oral hygiene doesn't just protect your teeth here; it's part of what lets you feel confident in the room.

The routine doesn't pause for celebration. If anything, celebration is exactly when it matters most.

02The seasonal tension

Then the festival arrives.

Everyday expert care holds all year. But Chinese New Year creates a specific, compressed problem — and it pushes from two directions at once.

Confidence demand spikes

Fifteen days of close contact. Every interaction happens at conversation distance, and most of them get photographed.

  • Greeting relatives face to face, over and over
  • Close-talk across crowded reunion tables
  • Group photos that live on family chats for years
  • Open-house rounds, back to back, all day
Collision

Sugar exposure spikes

At the exact same moment, the mouth is under more load than at any other point in the year.

  • Continuous grazing on festive sweets and cookies
  • The tray of togetherness, refilled and always open
  • Sweet drinks with every house visit
  • Late nights, irregular routines, skipped brushing

Two pressures. One mouth. Fifteen days. The campaign answers each one with its own game.

05Game 2 · answering Pillar B

Gathering Readiness

Pillar B · Defense

You start the game at 100% Sugar.

This is the whole idea, and it is set before the mechanic is even explained. The player does not begin clean and try to stay that way — they begin having already enjoyed the pineapple tarts, the love letters, the third round of the tray of togetherness. That is the realistic starting point of Chinese New Year, and pretending otherwise would make the game feel like a lecture.

So the goal is never abstaining. It is active defense and recovery from a state the player is already in — exactly the role Systema plays in a real festive week.

06Shared engagement shell

Two games. One shell around them.

Smile Swipe and Gathering Readiness are different mechanics answering different pillars — but they run on the same entry point, the same scoring furniture and the same reward loop. One build, one login, one leaderboard, one redemption flow.

That keeps production cost down, keeps the brand experience consistent across both halves of the message, and means the shell can be reused for the next festival without rebuilding it.
