# My Purrfriend 🐾

A cosy, real-time cat game based on a real brown-tabby-and-white cat. The cat lives on its own schedule using your device's clock. Check in, feed, pet and play whenever you like. The game never punishes you for being away.

## Play it

- **On your computer:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser, with nothing to install.
- **Put it online (optional):** drag this whole `cat-game` folder onto <https://app.netlify.com/drop>. Vercel or any other static host works the same way.

Your save stays in the browser on that device, so there's no login and no server.

## How to play

The game opens on your cat waiting in a cardboard box out in the garden, paws over the rim, grooming and watching your pointer. New players press **Start adoption**: the camera pulls back and a panel slides in where you name your cat, choose how it looks, set its tail (long, medium, short or a bunny bob, smooth or floofy, starting from what suits its breed) and pick a collar and extra (bell, bow tie, bandana or flower). Every change shows on the cat in the box. When you adopt, the garden fades into your living room around the cat and it hops out. Returning players see the garden once per browser session, then press **Continue**.

When choosing a look, pick a breed (Tabby & white, Calico, Tuxedo, Siamese, Ragdoll, Japanese Bobtail…), mix your own colour, pattern and white markings, or upload a photo, tap your cat's fur (and a stripe or patch, if it has one), and the game reads the coat for you. The photo is read on your device and isn't saved. Then you name your cat.

**The toys**

- **Feather wand and laser:** drag it around (or move your mouse). Wiggle it fast to make the cat pounce. The laser can't be caught.
- **Mouse on a string:** the mouse trails behind the string along the floor. The cat creeps up on it and, if you hold still, does the butt wiggle; yank the mouse away and it pounces. It can pin it under a paw (a squeak, and the mouse stays put for a second) or miss.
- **Ball launcher:** drag to aim (a dotted arc and a ring on the floor show where it will land) and let go to fire. The ball arcs, bounces off the walls and rolls to a stop. Then the cat decides, by its mood: **fetch** (it sprints, catches it, and either trots it back to you or keeps it: "Mine now."), **chase** (sets off, loses interest halfway and sits), **watch** (just turns its head), or **ignore** (turns its back and yawns). A happy, energetic cat, especially in the evening, fetches most of the time; a hungry, tired or grumpy one hardly ever does; a sleeping cat never does. A fetch is worth a little bond, and you can't fire while it's carrying the ball.
- **Bubble wand:** wave it to blow a few bubbles at a time (never more than nine in the air), which drift slowly down. The cat walks under the nearest one it can reach, leaps and pops it. Every pop is a little bond (capped per day like the other toys).

Animations worth watching for: at the bowl (or the cat grass) the cat dips its head and opens and shuts its mouth as it eats, and with a toy mouse, jingle ball, ball of yarn or fish kicker it knocks the toy to and fro with its front paws while the toy slides or rolls. The wall note always uses the cat's current name, even if you rename them after it went up.

Random little scenes the cat plays out by itself (each one also goes in the diary and counts toward an achievement):

- **Curtain climb.** A bored (unhappy) cat sprints to the left curtain, climbs halfway, hangs there swaying while the curtain swings, slides down in jerks and says nobody saw that. The lower the happiness, the likelier it is (5 times as likely when it's very low); it also raises happiness a little afterwards.
- **Butt wiggle, pounce, miss.** A moth appears. The cat crouches and wiggles its back end for two seconds, leaps straight past the moth, tumbles onto its back and says it meant to do that. Likeliest in the evening and at 3am.
- **Fell off the sill.** Watching the birds from the windowsill (or the cat tower), the cat leans a bit too far, wobbles, and tips off. It lands on its back, sits up, and starts grooming as if nothing happened. A tiny bit of pride is lost (happiness −2).
- **Paw in the water bowl.** The cat laps at the water, dips a front paw in, flicks it (water flies at its own face), squints, and thinks "Wet.".

The dotted orange rings that point things out (an empty bowl, a dirty litter box, a puddle, the diary, the wall note, the gachapon) only show until you've used that thing once. Gifts and knocked-over cups show up on the floor; tap them. Bond mostly goes up and it unlocks new food, toys and furniture. Each bit of bond floats up as a little "+1 ♥" by the bond bar. Bond comes from your daily check-in (+5), the days you spend together (+2), petting (20 a day), feeding and treats (8 a day), play (20 a day), invites (4 a day), care (4 a day, and +1 extra for the first scoop or clean-up each day), reading the day's note (+1), achievements (+2, or +5 for the big ones, once you claim them) and gifts. The levels need 25, 60, 110, 180, 270, 390, 540, 740 and 1000 points. A grumpy cat can take a little back, but never below the start of your current level, so nothing you've unlocked is lost.

### Promo codes

Players enter a code in **Settings → Promo code**, or from the **Have a code?** card at the end of the shop. A code can give **cat coins**, **free gachapon spins** (used before coins, shown as "Free spin (n left)") and **shop looks** (a look the player already owns is paid as its price in coins instead). Several rewards can come in one code and are paid together.

How a code is checked, in order: tidy the text (capitals only, spaces and dashes ignored) → empty? → in a break? → a real code? → started? → ended? → already used on this device? → pay out and save together. A wrong code counts as a try; after **5 wrong tries in a row** entry locks for **5 minutes** (a countdown that survives reloads). "Not active yet" and "ended" don't count as wrong tries. If the browser can't save, nothing is paid and the code isn't used up. Used codes and the try count live in their own storage key (`catgame.promo.v1`) and **survive Start over**, so a restart can't claim a code again. Dates use the real clock, so Testing tools' "skip time" can't open or extend a code.

There is no server, so a code works **once per device**. It can't be limited to "the first 100 people", and a leaked code can be used once on every device. Use an end date and modest rewards.

**To add a code**, add a line to `CODES` in `js/core/promo.js`:

1. Get its hash: open the game, then in the browser console run `CatGame.Promo.hash('SPRING-24')`.
2. Add `{ h: '<that hash>', from: '2026-10-01', to: '2026-12-31', rewards: [{ coins: 50 }, { spins: 2 }, { look: 'rug:flower' }] }`. Look ids are `piece:style` from `js/core/shop.js`.
3. Publish the game. To end a code early, change its `to` date or remove the line.

Only the hash is stored, so the code can't just be read from the file. The one code that ships, `WELCOME-PURR` (20 coins and a free spin), is a sample: replace it before you share the game.

### Testing without waiting

Tap the name card, then **Settings → Testing tools**:

- **+1 hour / +6 hours / +1 day** pretends time passed, so you see the "While you were away…" summary straight away.
- **Seasonal decor preview** shows the Chinese New Year, Hari Raya or Christmas decorations at any time of year.

To check the game logic without a browser: `node cat-game/tests/core-check.mjs`.

## Make it your cat

Edit `js/core/profile.js`: the default name, the description, and the quirks. Breed presets and coat colours live in `js/core/coats.js`. The quirks are picked from the photos: the round scratcher bed, loafing on the table, supervising the TV, the camping chair, and yelling for breakfast. Turn any of them off, or make them stronger.

## How it's built

Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no build step. The art style (Neko Atsume–inspired: thick ink lines, flat colours, round cats) is defined in `docs/art-style.md`; follow it when drawing anything new. The **game logic** (`js/core`) never touches the screen, so it can be reused as-is in a phone app later. Only `js/ui` would change.

### Time system

- Game time is the device's local time (plus an optional test offset).
- The cat always has one **activity** (nap, groom, knock the cup…) with a start and end time. When it ends, the brain picks the next one. The choice is weighted by time of day (dawn, morning, afternoon, evening, night, plus 3am chaos), by needs (hunger, energy), mood, quirks and favourites.
- **Offline catch-up:** when you return, the same brain replays every activity from your last visit until now, using long "away" durations. Each choice is seeded by its timestamp, so the replay is the same every time. Stats drift along the way. The cat eats from the bowl, brings gifts and knocks things over. Then the summary is built from that replay.
- While you're watching, activities are shorter (seconds to a few minutes), so the cat feels alive.

### Data model (the save)

`stats` (fullness, energy, happiness, bond, litter need) · `activity` · `bowl` · `room` (slots, hammock, cup, gifts, litter box) · `unlocked` · `favorites` · `log` (last 3 days of events) · `journal` (diary entries, each keeping the name used at the time) · `treasures` · `cat.nameHistory` · `settings`. Photos are stored separately.

### Low-maintenance rules

- Stats drift slowly. Fullness takes about 20 hours to run low, and the cat feeds itself from the bowl.
- Nothing can die, get sick or run away. Happiness has a floor; worst case is a grumpy cat.
- Bond only drops by a point or two when you upset the cat (tail, paws, waking it), and never below the start of the current level. It also grows a little every day, even on days you don't visit.
