Style samples for approval

These follow docs/art-style.md. Our own tabby-and-white cat is drawn in a Neko Atsume–inspired style. Nothing here is traced or copied from Hit-Point art. All drawings are inline SVG, built the same way the game draws them, so whatever you approve here ports straight across.

2 · Poses

v3: rounder bodies, smaller legs and paws. v2, simplified: head and ears are one shape, eyes are solid ink dots, bigger dot eyes, an ink oval nose sits on top of the ω mouth, there are no whiskers, a thin mint collar peeks out under the chin, and the coat has just two forehead bars and one or two bars per side. Same head in every pose; the body does the acting. The feet sit at (0,0), matching the current code's convention.
